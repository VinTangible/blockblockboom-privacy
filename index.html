BlockBlockBoom Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

I’m Vincent Tang, an independent developer based in the United States, publishing BlockBlockBoom as VinTangible. This policy explains the information used by the game, its advertising and privacy services, and how to contact me.

Privacy contact: blockblockboom@gmail.com

Information saved on your device

The game saves your high score, in-game Currency, Themes, Power-Ups, tutorial progress, and sound and vibration preferences locally. It also saves the current run's game state. These records allow the game to restore saved progress.

The game also keeps one previously committed local save as backup. At startup, if the main save is missing or damaged and the backup save is valid, the game restores the backup. An older backup may not include recent progress. Related local files support saving and recovery.

The game stores local records of ad rewards and displays to prevent duplicate rewards and limit how often ads are shown. Unsaved progress or rewards may be lost if the app closes before saving finishes.

These save and recovery files remain on your device; the game does not automatically send them to me. Local game records do not have a fixed automatic deletion period. Some recovery files are replaced or removed when they are no longer needed.

Your device's backup or transfer features may restore saved progress when you replace your phone. Availability depends on your device, settings and available backups; recent progress may be missing or no save may return. The game has no sign-in or custom game-save cloud service.

Removing app data removes local game saves. Device backups may retain or restore copies under their own settings and retention practices. Removing local data does not automatically delete information already held by advertising or other service providers.

Ads and the information they use

BlockBlockBoom uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) handles advertising privacy choices. The game does not request personalized ads or remarketing.

Google's advertising and privacy services may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, information about app and ad interactions, and technical performance information. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. This information supports ad delivery, reporting, security and fraud prevention. Advertising partners involved in serving an ad may also process information; the information and recipients depend on the services and ad involved.

UMP communicates with Google to check for required privacy messages and handle your choices. This can happen before you make a choice. Privacy choices and signals are stored locally and supplied to applicable advertising services. See Google's privacy policy.

Your advertising choices

The app checks whether a privacy form is required before your first game. When a form is shown, it explains the choices available to you. You can play whether you accept or refuse optional advertising choices. A failed privacy check offers a retry.

When available, open Settings → Privacy choices to review or change your choices. You can also manage advertising-related permissions through your device settings. Your choices can affect which ads are available.

Even when ads are not personalized, advertising services may use device information and local storage for ad delivery, frequency limits, reporting, security and fraud prevention. Refusing optional choices does not necessarily stop every privacy-service or permitted advertising operation.

Changing a choice does not automatically delete information already held by service providers. See the section below for information about requests and provider retention.

If you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you include, such as your name, message or attachments. I use this information to answer your question or handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your question.

I use Gmail to receive and store these messages. I am the only person with access to the support mailbox, and I protect the account with two-factor authentication. Google processes messages as the email service provider.

I delete resolved support conversations within 90 days after resolution. You can request earlier deletion by emailing me. I honor these requests unless a specific legal need requires me to retain information. Deletion includes relevant messages in my inbox and sent mail, and removal from Trash. Google manages remaining copies in its systems under its own retention practices.

Data held by service providers

Deleting the app, resetting a local choice or changing advertising permission does not automatically delete information already held by service providers. Google explains its handling, controls and retention criteria in its privacy policy and retention information. These general pages do not specify one retention period for every advertising or UMP record.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of information, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Email blockblockboom@gmail.com to ask about your information or applicable rights. I have no BlockBlockBoom server copy of your local saves to delete. Requests about provider-held information may require additional information to locate the relevant records.

Intended audience

BlockBlockBoom is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not specifically directed to children under 13. The game does not ask you to provide your age. Parents or guardians can contact blockblockboom@gmail.com with questions about a child's information.

Changes and contact
